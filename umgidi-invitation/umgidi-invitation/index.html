Uyamenywa ngembeko · You are cordially invited

Intsapho yakwaVice

Ngovuyo olukhulu, intsapho yakwaVice iyakumema ukuba uzokubhiyozela kunye nathi umgidi wonyana wethu, uOlwethu “Oza” Vice. Ugqibile uhambo lwakhe lobudoda entabeni, kwaye ngoku ubuyela ekhaya njengekrwala.

With great joy, the Vice family cordially invites you to celebrate the umgidi of our son, Olwethu “Oza” Vice. He has completed his journey to manhood on the mountain and now returns home as ikrwala, a new man.

Yiza nentliziyo evuyayo. Masimamkele ekhaya ngeengoma, ngomdaniso nangeengcebiso zabadala.

Come with a joyful heart, as we welcome him home with song, dance and the wisdom of the elders.

Camagu!
