Uyamenywa ngembeko

Ngovuyo olukhulu, usapho lakwaVice, AmaNgxongo, OoZondwa, OoNtsundu, OoBhomoyi liyanimema ukuba nizokubhiyoza kunye nabo umgidi wonyana wabo, uOlwethu “Oza” Vice. Ugqibile uhambo lwakhe lobudoda entabeni, kwaye ngoku ubuyela ekhaya njengekrwala.

You are cordially invited with great joy, the Vice family cordially invites you to celebrate the umgidi of our son, Olwethu “Oza” Vice. He has completed his journey to manhood on the mountain and now returns home as ikrwala, a new man.

Yiza nentliziyo evuyayo. Masimamkele ekhaya ngeengoma, ngomdaniso nangeengcebiso zabadala.

Come with a joyful heart, as we welcome him home with song, dance and the wisdom of the elders.

Camagu!
